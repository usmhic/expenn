expenn keeps your travel spending organized — from the first receipt capture on the road to final reimbursement back at the office, or just your own personal expense log for a solo trip.

<Callout type="info">
  This Help Center is written for the people *using* expenn — travelers, team
  managers, and workspace admins. If you're setting up the server for the first
  time, start with the [README on GitHub](https://github.com/usmhic/expenn).
</Callout>

## What expenn does

expenn has two sides that work together:

**For travelers** — capture receipts on the go, log expenses against a trip, store travel documents, and submit for reimbursement when you're back.

**For finance & admins** — create and assign trips, review submitted expenses, approve or reject with a note, and track team spend across all trips from one dashboard.
