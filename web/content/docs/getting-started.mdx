title: Getting started
description: Sign in, pick your account type, and set up your workspace in under two minutes
---

## 1 — Sign in

Go to `/login` and enter your email address. expenn sends you a **one-time code** — no password to remember. Enter the 6-digit code to sign in or create your account automatically.

<Callout type="info">
  If your company uses Google or Apple SSO, your admin will have configured
  those options — you'll see them as buttons below the email field.
</Callout>

## 2 — Choose your account type

First-time users land on the **onboarding** screen. You'll see two options:

<Cards>
  <Card title="Personal" description="For solo travelers or freelancers — track your own expenses. One-click setup, no invites needed." href="/docs/auth-and-roles" />
  <Card title="Company" description="For teams — name your workspace and start inviting teammates. You become the workspace owner." href="/docs/auth-and-roles" />
</Cards>

If you were **invited to an existing workspace**, you'll see a pending invitation instead — accept it to join as the assigned role.

## 3 — Explore your dashboard

After setup you land in your workspace:

- **Traveler home** (`/[workspace]/traveler`) — your active trip card, recent expenses, and quick-capture button
- **Admin dashboard** (`/[workspace]/admin`) — finance overview, pending approvals, and team stats (admins and owners)

## What's next?

<Steps>
  <Step>
    **Create a trip.** Go to Trips → New trip. Set dates, destination, budget, and currency.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Invite your team** (company only). Settings → Members → Invite. Pick a role — Admin, Manager, or Traveler.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Capture your first expense.** Use the mobile app's camera tab or the web "New expense" form, link it to a trip.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    **Submit for review.** Travelers submit draft expenses; admins and managers see them in the Expenses review queue.
  </Step>
</Steps>
