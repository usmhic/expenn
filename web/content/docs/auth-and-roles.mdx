## Signing in

expenn uses **passwordless OTP (magic code)** authentication — no passwords to set or forget.

<Steps>
  <Step>Enter your email address on the sign-in page.</Step>
  <Step>Check your inbox for a 6-digit code (arrives in under 30 seconds).</Step>
  <Step>Enter the code to sign in. First-time users are automatically registered.</Step>
</Steps>

Google and Apple SSO are also supported for workspaces that have configured them.

## Account types

### Personal

Created when you choose **"Manage Personal Expenses"** on onboarding (or sign in via the mobile app for the first time).

- Single-user workspace — no team, no invitations
- Flat billing: **$7/month** to unlock the full feature set (free tier has basic access)
- Good for freelancers, solo travelers, or anyone tracking personal spend

### Company

Created when you choose **"Manage Company Expenses"** on onboarding.

- Multi-user workspace with teams, roles, and an approval workflow
- Free plan: **2 seats** (owner + 1 additional member)
- Paid plan: **$7/month per additional member** beyond the 2 included

## Roles

| Role | Where you see it | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner** | Company workspace creator | Everything — billing, settings, all members, all trips |
| **Admin** | Invited by owner | All trips & expenses for the org, invite members, no billing |
| **Manager** | Invited by admin/owner | Their team's trips and expenses only — no org settings or billing |
| **Traveler** | Invited by admin/owner | Own expenses, documents, and trips they've been assigned to |

### Manager scope

Managers see and act on **their team only**:
- Can review and approve expenses for travelers in their team
- Can create and manage trips assigned to their team
- Cannot see other teams' data, billing, analytics, or org-wide settings
- Appear in the admin nav but with a reduced sidebar (no Analytics link)

## Inviting members

Owners and admins can invite members from **Settings → Members → Invite**.

1. Enter the invitee's email address
2. Choose a role: **Traveler**, **Manager**, or **Admin**
3. An invitation email is sent with a secure link (expires in 7 days)

The invitee clicks the link, signs in (or registers), and lands directly in your workspace.

<Callout type="warn">
  Each paid seat beyond the 2 included in the free plan adds **$7/month** to
  your workspace bill. You'll see a payment-required error if you try to invite
  someone when all paid seats are in use — manage your subscription in the
  Billing section first.
</Callout>

## Switching workspaces

If you belong to multiple workspaces, use the workspace switcher in the top-left corner. Clicking **Switch workspace** shows all your memberships — select one to make it active.

Server actions and API calls always use your **active organization**. Switching is instant and persists across sessions.
