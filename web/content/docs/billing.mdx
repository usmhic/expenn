expenn billing is handled by **Paddle** (Merchant of Record) — they manage invoicing, tax, and payment processing globally.

## Free plan

Every new workspace starts on the free plan:

| | Personal | Company |
|---|---|---|
| Seats | 1 (you) | 2 (owner + 1) |
| Trips | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Expenses | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Documents | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Billing | Flat $7/mo to upgrade | $7/mo per seat beyond 2 |

## Personal Pro plan

If you're on a **personal account**, upgrading unlocks the full feature set for a flat rate:

> **$7 / month** — one seat, all features, no per-user pricing.

Go to **Account → Upgrade** (the Account link appears in your traveler nav when you're on a personal workspace).

## Company per-seat billing

Company workspaces include **2 seats free** (owner + 1). Each additional member costs:

> **$7 / month per additional member**

When you try to invite a member beyond the 2 included and you haven't subscribed, you'll see a payment-required prompt. Click it to open the checkout page, enter how many additional seats you need, and complete payment.

**Example:** a 5-person team costs $21/month (3 paid seats × $7).

## Managing your subscription

All billing is handled through **Paddle**. After subscribing you can:
- **Add seats** — go to `/checkout` and subscribe with a higher seat count, or manage directly in Paddle's customer portal
- **Cancel** — cancel any time; your plan stays active until the end of the billing period
- **View invoices** — Paddle emails invoices automatically after each charge

<Callout type="info">
  The final price shown at checkout may differ slightly from the base rate — Paddle
  calculates taxes automatically based on your location before charging.
</Callout>

## Billing status

Your workspace's billing status is visible in **Settings → Billing**:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `free` | On the free plan |
| `trialing` | Active trial (if applicable) |
| `active` | Paid and current |
| `past_due` | Payment failed — update payment method in Paddle |
| `canceled` | Subscription ended |
