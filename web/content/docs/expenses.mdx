---
title: Expenses
description: Capturing receipts, submitting expenses, and the review queue
---

Expenses are the core of expenn. A traveler creates them, optionally attaches a receipt photo, and submits them for review. An admin or manager approves or rejects — and the traveler gets notified.

## Creating an expense

### On web

Go to **My expenses → New expense**. Fill in:

- **Merchant** — who you paid (required)
- **Amount & currency** (required)
- **Date** — defaults to today
- **Category** — Travel, Accommodation, Meals, Transport, Communication, Office, Entertainment, Other
- **Trip** — the trip this expense belongs to (optional but recommended)
- **Notes** — any context for the reviewer
- **Receipt** — upload a photo or PDF of the receipt

The expense is created as a **draft** — it won't appear in the review queue until you submit it.

### On mobile

Use the **Scan** tab — point the camera at a receipt, fill in the details on the form below, and tap **Save expense**. The camera captures a photo and attaches it automatically.

## Expense statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | Created but not submitted — only visible to you |
| `submitted` | Sent for review — visible to admins and managers |
| `approved` | Accepted by a reviewer |
| `rejected` | Rejected with a note — edit and resubmit if needed |
| `reimbursed` | Payment confirmed — fully resolved |

## Submitting expenses

From the expense list, tap **Submit** on any draft expense. You can also bulk-submit all drafts at once with the **Submit all drafts** button.

<Callout type="warn">
  Once submitted you can no longer edit the expense. If you need to make a
  change, ask your admin to reject it — then edit and resubmit.
</Callout>

## The review queue (admin & manager)

Go to **Expenses** in the admin nav. All submitted expenses appear here, sorted by submission date. For each expense you can:

- **Approve** — marks as approved, notifies the traveler
- **Reject** — requires a rejection reason, sends the expense back to draft
- **View receipt** — opens the attached image or PDF
- **Filter** by traveler, trip, status, category, or date range

Managers only see expenses from travelers in their team.

## Missing receipts

Any expense without an attached receipt shows a warning on the traveler's mobile home screen. The **Missing** stat chip on the home screen shows a count — tap it to go to the expense list pre-filtered to missing-receipt items.

## Analytics

The **Analytics** page (admin/owner only) shows:
- Total spend by trip, traveler, and category
- Month-over-month trend
- Pending vs. approved breakdown
