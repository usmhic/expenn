title: Expenses

Expenses are the core of expenn. A traveler captures a receipt, submits it, and a reviewer approves it and marks it paid. Every step is visible to both sides, so nobody has to ask "where's my money?".

```
draft ──▶ submitted ──▶ approved ──▶ paid (reimbursed)
              │
              └──▶ sent back (rejected) ──▶ fix ──▶ resubmit
```

## Creating an expense

### On web

Use **Capture** in the sidebar or **Add expense** on the Expenses page. The form starts with the receipt:

- **Receipt** — tap to take a photo or choose a file, or drag a photo or PDF onto the drop zone
- **Amount & currency** — the currency follows the trip you pick, unless you change it
- **Merchant** and **Date** — the date defaults to today
- **Category** — Meals, Lodging, Airfare, Ground transport, Mileage, Fuel, Conference & training, Client entertainment, Office & supplies, Software, Phone & internet, Other
- **Trip** — your active trip is selected for you; choose **No trip** for everyday spend
- **Paid with** — choosing **Company card** turns off **Pay me back**, because the company already paid
- **Notes** — business purpose, attendees, anything the reviewer needs

Then choose **Save draft** to finish later, or **Submit for review** to send it now. On the Capture page the form clears after each save so you can work through a stack of receipts.

### On mobile

Use the **Scan** tab — point the camera at a receipt, fill in the details, and tap **Save expense**.

## Expense statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | Saved but not submitted — only visible to you; you can edit or delete it |
| `submitted` | Waiting for a reviewer — it can't be edited while it is being reviewed |
| `rejected` | Sent back with a reason — edit it and resubmit |
| `approved` | Accepted; reimbursable expenses now count as **owed to you** |
| `reimbursed` | Paid out — fully resolved |

## Submitting and fixing

- **Submit** sends one draft. **Submit all** on the Expenses page sends every draft at once.
- When a reviewer sends an expense back, it appears in red with their reason, and the bell shows it. Click **Fix**, make the change, and click **Resubmit**.
- The tiles at the top of the Expenses page show what is **owed to you**, what is **in review**, and what has been **paid back**. Amounts in different currencies are shown side by side, never added together.

## Review & pay (admins and managers)

Open **Review** in the admin sidebar. The page has five tabs:

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **To review** | Submitted expenses, oldest first |
| **To pay** | Approved expenses that haven't been paid yet |
| **Paid** | Reimbursed expenses |
| **Sent back** | Rejected expenses the traveler still has to fix |
| **All** | Everything that has been submitted |

To decide on several expenses at once, tick them (or use **Select all**) and choose **Approve**, **Send back**, or **Mark paid**. **Select … without issues** ticks every expense that has a receipt and no duplicate warning, so routine items take one click.

Sending an expense back always asks for a reason — the traveler sees it next to the expense.

### Review hints

expenn flags expenses that deserve a second look. Flags never block a decision.

- **No receipt** — nothing is attached.
- **Possible duplicate** — the same person has another expense with the same amount, currency, and date.

### Paying out and exporting

After you've paid travelers (through payroll or a bank transfer batch), select the expenses on **To pay** and choose **Mark paid**. **Export CSV** downloads the expenses in the current tab, with traveler, email, category, amount, currency, reviewer, review note, and timestamps. Spreadsheets, QuickBooks, and Xero can import the file.

## Analytics

The **Analytics** page (admins and owners) shows, for one year and one currency at a time:

- Approved spend, with what is still waiting for review
- Budget used across trips, with an **Over budget** warning
- **Time to decision** and **time to reimburse** — how long travelers wait
- Monthly spend, top categories, top spenders, and each trip against its budget
