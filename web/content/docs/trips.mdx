title: Trips
description: Creating trips, assigning travelers, tracking progress and spend
---

A **trip** is the container for a travel event — it has a destination, date range, currency, and optional budget. Expenses are attached to trips, which is how spend gets tracked and reported.

## Creating a trip

Go to **Trips → New trip**. You'll need:

- **Name** — e.g. "Q3 Sales Summit – Berlin"
- **Destination** — city or country
- **Start & end dates**
- **Currency** — the primary currency for expenses on this trip
- **Budget** (optional) — enables the budget-vs-actual progress bar

Trips can only be created by **Admins**, **Managers** (for their team), and **Owners**.

## Trip statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | Created but not started — travelers can be assigned |
| `active` | In progress — travelers are on the road |
| `completed` | Trip ended — expenses can still be submitted |
| `archived` | Closed — read-only |

Admins and managers move trips between statuses from the trip detail page. Travelers cannot change trip status.

## Assigning travelers

From the trip detail page, use **Assign travelers** to add workspace members to a trip. Assigned travelers see the trip in their mobile home screen and can attach expenses to it.

<Callout type="info">
  Personal-account users don't have an "assign travelers" step — all trips
  belong to themselves automatically.
</Callout>

## Trip progress

The trip detail card shows:
- **Date progress** — how far through the travel window you are (as a percentage)
- **Budget vs. actual** — total approved expenses against the trip budget (if set)
- **Pending expenses** — expenses awaiting review

## Approvals on trips

Once a traveler submits expenses for a trip, they appear in the **Expenses → Review queue**. Approving or rejecting expenses from that queue automatically updates the trip's spend total.

## Trips in the mobile app

The traveler's **Home** tab always surfaces the active trip in the hero card. The **Trips** tab shows all trips with search and status filters — tap any trip to see its full details, expense breakdown, and timeline.
