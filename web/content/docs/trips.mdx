title: Trips & route planning
description: Create trips, plan multi-stop routes on the map, assign travelers and track spend

A **trip** brings everything about a journey together: dates, budget and currency, who's travelling, the approval, the route and every expense logged against it.

## Creating a trip

Choose **Trips → New trip** and fill in:

- **Trip name** — e.g. "Berlin client summit"
- **Main destination** — city or region
- **Start and end dates**
- **Budget** (optional) and **currency** — the budget bar on the trip page counts expenses in this currency
- **Team** and **who's travelling** (company workspaces)

When you select **Create and plan route**, the trip opens on its map so you can add stops right away.

Who can create trips:

| Workspace | Who |
|---|---|
| Company | Owners, admins, and managers (for their own teams) |
| Personal | You — you're added to every trip you create |

## Planning the route

Every trip page has an **itinerary** next to an interactive map.

1. **Add stops.** Type a city, address or venue into **Add a stop**, or click anywhere on the map and select **Add**.
2. **Order them.** Drag stops by the handle to reorder them. With four or more stops, **Optimise order** finds the shortest visiting order. It keeps your first and last stops fixed.
3. **Choose how you travel.** Pick car, van/truck, bike or walking above the stop list. The route redraws, with the distance and travel time for each leg.
4. **Compare routes.** With exactly two stops you may get alternatives. Select one in the route chips or click its dashed line on the map.
5. **Add details.** Open a stop to set its type (stop, overnight stay, meeting, transit), arrival and departure dates, and notes such as a booking reference.
6. **Save the itinerary.** Everyone on the trip sees the saved route. Trip cards across the app show a thumbnail of it, the stops, and the total distance.

Travelers assigned to the trip, managers of its team, and admins can edit the itinerary.

### Claiming mileage

When the route is driven, travelers can select **Claim mileage** under the route total. This opens a prefilled expense: the merchant reads "Mileage: start → end", the category is Mileage, and the notes record the distance. Enter the amount using your organisation's mileage rate, then submit as usual.

<Callout type="info">
  Place search and routing are powered by [openrouteservice](https://openrouteservice.org), and the maps use OpenStreetMap data.
  If your administrator hasn't set this up, you can still pin stops by clicking the map. Distances and routes won't be calculated until it's configured.
</Callout>

## Trip statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `draft` | Being set up — not yet visible as an active trip |
| `active` | Planned or in progress |
| `completed` | Trip ended — expenses can still be submitted |
| `archived` | Closed — read-only |

## Travel approval

In company workspaces, travelers select **Request approval** on the trip page and add a short purpose. Admins and managers see the request in three places:

- **Overview**
- the top of **Trips**
- the trip's **Travelers & approvals** panel

From any of these they can approve or decline it. Several requests can be approved at once from the **Trips** page.

## Budget and spend

The trip page shows:

- **Spent / approved spend** against the budget, with the amount still in review
- **Route** distance and travel time
- **Expenses** on the trip, with their status
- **Reimbursement** — what's approved and still due to each traveler (admins)

## Setting up routing (administrators)

Routing and place search need an [openrouteservice API key](https://openrouteservice.org/dev/#/signup). The free plan is enough for most teams. Set `OpenRouteService__ApiKey` for the API and restart it. To use a self-hosted openrouteservice instance, also set `OpenRouteService__BaseUrl`.

Map tiles come from [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org) by default. To use your own MapLibre style, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAP_STYLE_URL` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAP_STYLE_URL_DARK` when building the web app.
